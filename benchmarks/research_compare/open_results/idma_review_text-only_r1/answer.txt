- The packet FIFO is configured for depth 5 and fall-through at [`integration.sv:39`](\/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/idma_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/integration.sv:39). Its three-bit usage output is also the correct width for values 0 through 5.

- There is a definite SystemVerilog type-elaboration blocker. `cc_stream_fifo` correctly receives `beat_t`, but passes `.data_t(DataWidth)` to `cc_fifo` at [`cc_stream_fifo.sv:54`](\/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/idma_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/rtl/.bender/git/checkouts/common_cells-b5ef1d9e9e7e3ce8/src/cc_stream_fifo.sv:54). `DataWidth` is an `int unsigned` value parameter, not a type, so it is not a legal override of `cc_fifo`’s type parameter. Change that one connection to `.data_t(data_t)`. That preserves the complete `beat_t` through `compare_idma_state.i_packet_fifo.fifo_i` without changing the top-level interface, depth, or fall-through setting.

- Flush behavior violates the stated storage requirement. `cc_fifo` combines clear and flush into `clr_or_flush` at [`cc_fifo.sv:120`](\/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/idma_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/rtl/.bender/git/checkouts/common_cells-b5ef1d9e9e7e3ce8/src/cc_fifo.sv:120), appropriately resetting pointers and occupancy. But it also uses that combined signal to clear `mem_q` at [`cc_fifo.sv:126`](\/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/idma_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/rtl/.bender/git/checkouts/common_cells-b5ef1d9e9e7e3ce8/src/cc_fifo.sv:126). The register macro gives clear priority over a write enable, so a flush edge zeroes payload storage even with no push or pop. Replace only the memory macro’s clear argument with `clr_i`; retain `clr_or_flush` for the pointers and count. Then clear still zeroes storage, reset still may zero it, and flush only empties the logical queue while allowing an enabled write to update storage.

- The scalar beat handshake is not enforced atomically across the three lane FIFOs. [`ready_o`](\/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/idma_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/integration.sv:29) is the AND of lane readies, but each lane receives raw `valid_i` at [`integration.sv:35`](\/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/idma_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/integration.sv:35). Likewise, each lane may pop on raw `packet_ready` at [`integration.sv:36`](\/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/idma_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/integration.sv:36), even if not all lane valids are asserted. If lane states ever differ, a lane can accept or discard data while the corresponding whole-beat interface has no handshake. The symmetric local implementation should remain lockstep after a normal reset, but that is an implicit invariant rather than interface enforcement. For robust compliance, drive lane valids with `valid_i & (&lane_ready)` and lane readies with `packet_ready & (&lane_valid)`.

The clear/flush controls only target the packet FIFO. The lane buffers are hard-wired not to clear or flush. That is consistent with the stated packet-FIFO-only requirements, but if system intent is to discard all staged beats, additional control wiring would be needed.
