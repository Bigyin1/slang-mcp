- The W FIFO supplies `.dtype(Depth)` rather than `.dtype(w_chan_t)` ([axi_fifo.sv:100–115](/private/tmp/slang-compare-open/runs/axi_review/text-only/rep_1/workspace/rtl/src/axi_fifo.sv:100)). With the expected `fifo_v3` type parameter, this is a type/value-category error; it also fails the requirement that the W type be preserved.
