Requirements:
- Each beat contains three packed lanes; each lane contains an 8-bit payload, 4-bit tag and one poison bit. Preserve those lane types and the complete beat through the generated lane buffers and the packet FIFO.
- The integration requests five entries per lane buffer and five packet entries. Respect the declared valid/ready interfaces and retain the packet FIFO's configured fall-through behavior.
- For the packet FIFO, clear_packet_i clears queue pointers, occupancy and stored payload. flush_packet_i empties the queue by resetting pointers and occupancy. A flush must not itself clear payload storage; ordinary enabled writes may still update it. In particular, with clear_packet_i low and no push or pop, a flush edge must leave payload storage unchanged. A low rst_ni may reset storage.
